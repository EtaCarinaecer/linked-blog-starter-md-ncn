[[Craiul]], for both economic, social, historical, and navigational purposes, is separated into Deltas, or sectors. These were once smaller regions and villages that sprawled into the main city upon their isolation from the outside world, but wished to maintain some form of autonomy.

Although they each have their own unique names, usually only people local to that particular region call it that, whilst everyone else simply refers to it by number.

Each Delta has its own representative body, be it an oligarch, be it a democratically elected mayor, who is directly responsible for all that is bureaucracy concerning their region, and who stands before the [[Clergy]] in all matters concerning their location.
## [[Delta 1]] - New Chicago
This is the northeastern inner circle of the city, bordering [[Lake Augusta]] as well as the core industrial sector. It's characterized by its large, glassy skyscrapers, and a building called the Space Elevator — a structure designed to transport people to the very top of the dome. 
Ruler: role, name
## [[Delta 2]] - Bluegrove
This is the southeastern bank of Lake Augusta, supervising a majority of the city's masonry, mines, as well as sporting the highest density of hospitals. Its primary trades are smithing, weapon-, and armorcraft, but the majority of gadgets are all manufactured here.
Ruler: Nikit, elected mayor
## [[Delta 3]] - Lakeside
Southwestern bank of Augusta, this region primarily occupies itself with being the actual food exporter and provider for the city, bordering the majority of its fields, farms, and orchards. The southern edge is where most of clothing is commercialized, the western edge is where woodcraft is handled.
This region is which [[Augusta]] jurisdictionally belongs to, and it handles matters pertaining to the lake's residents.
Ruler: role, name (someone from the lake)
## [[Delta 4]] - Heavens' Rest
Northwestern border to Augusta, this is part of the entertainment sector, with a higher focus on looks, vibes, and shopping malls. It really just serves as a tamer entry point to the Forest.
Ruler: role, name (Nae, de facto)
## [[Delta 5]] - Snowgold
Northern edge of the city, on the west bordered by the Spider Forest, a majority of its space is taken up by residences, with living wages descending from southwest towards northeast.
The location's PR rivalry with Silverpine is generational.
Ruler: X.C, monarch?
## [[Delta 6]] - Old Hearth
Eastern side, housing the famous [[Kirkolisch]] dam, and providing a transition from residential regions to the mining and masonry sector. Industrialized with many factories and heavy machinery, poorer roads, and it's where a lot of the minimum wage workers spend their daily 6. The clergy pays immense effort to keep the air clean in this region.
Elementals live here.
Ruler: Bee, elected but not mayor
## [[Delta 7]] - Riverbend
The agricultural sector, south of the city, that covers exports of all kinds from near tropical, to the southern edge managing to emulate the crops of a tempered region. Most of the residents live up on the pillars, and travel to work by elevator or gondola. There are close to no buildings on ground level, especially not designated for living in.
That, except a large villa.
Ruler: Sky, elected mayor
## [[Delta 8]] - Silverpine
West of the city, it houses further orchards, as well as the Spider Forest. This is the heart of money exchange in Craiul, with the dark of said forest only seeing light in the form of neons from malls and casinos. Pillar Ferris is overgrown with vines, creating an atmospheric ride for anyone rich enough to live in this sector -- of course, up on the support beam and pillar.  Some say the dark keeps the Clergy's gaze away. This region is responsible for the majority of goods trades with the [[Underground]]. There is a regional rivalry in terms of PR with Snowgold.
Ruler: Nae, oligarch
