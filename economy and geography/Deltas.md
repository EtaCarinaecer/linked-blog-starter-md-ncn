Craiul, for both economic, social, historical, and navigational purposes, is separated into Deltas, or sectors. These were once smaller regions and villages that sprawled into the main city upon their isolation from the outside world, but wished to maintain some form of autonomy.

Although they each have their own unique names, usually only people local to that particular region call it that, whilst everyone else simply refers to it by number.

Each Delta has its own representative body, be it an oligarch, be it a democratically elected mayor, who is directly responsible for all that is bureaucracy concerning their region, and who stands before the Clergy in all matters concerning their location.
## Delta 1 - New Chicago
This is the northeastern inner circle of the city, bordering Lake Augusta as well as the core industrial sector. It's characterized by its large, glassy skyscrapers, and a building called the Space Elevator — a structure designed to transport people to the very top of the dome.
