Known as the Crystal Eye, or the crown jewel of [[Craiul]], Lake Augusta is a cold, clear blue, freshwater lake. It is a gathering basin for the [[Kirkolisch]] river.
## Structure
Lake Augusta is an artificial lake exploiting the remnants of a manmade wound in the earth. The surface water is about as cold as Kirkolisch's, at barely above freezing, but due to its immense depths, the bottom is significantly warmer -- provided, no person has ventured yet to explore its very bottom in detail.
Its current measured deepest point is at 3452 m.
`It was sealed off from the hall system that Hanykatek used, by rubble. A lot of shit can be found here from Hanyka.`
The very bottom has a faint glow, from several enchanted crystals put in place by the [[Clergy]] with the purpose of keeping the lake clean.
## Wildlife
Lake Augusta houses a large community, which jurisdictionally belongs to [[Delta 3]].
The lake has no natural wildlife, as river Kirkolisch itself seems to have no life in it that wasn't imported and brought in by Craiul's citizens.
The eastern bank is generally not habitable due to waste and industrial pollution.
## Entertainment
Boating and fishing are permitted during certain parts of the year, at certain locations bordering the west bank of the lake.
