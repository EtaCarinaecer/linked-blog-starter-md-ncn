The beginning of the end, the birth of a new calendar, the great divide -- the Cataclysm is known as many things, yet somehow the collective memory failed to retain its exact details.

One thing is for certain: The Cataclysm was wrought by the folly of man. Having gone too far with their research and what science alone could support, a discovery was made from which there was no return. What this means exactly depends on who is telling the story: some say it was an experiment gone wrong. Some say it was a bomb. Some say, it was when man created magic.

Craiul spent nearly a thousand years recovering from its effects. It was nothing short of a hard reset on life within the bubble, and with their utter isolation from the outside world, it was the uphill battle of uphill battles. Monsters roaming free and fighting for their space within the city, the scarcity of resources, the complete eradication of a number of pillars of humanity, saw humans returning to the invention of fire and wheels. At least, this time around, they had the advantage of a collective knowledge, and a vast archive of information, and soon Craiul began to show the first signs of progress.

Lake Augusta is rumored to be the heart of the devastation. This is theorized to be the reason it supports no native life (neither does lake Kirkolisch for that matter), though scientific study into it found no supporting evidence of this fact.
