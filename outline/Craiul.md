The Crowned City of Craiul is a sovereign citystate, often touted as the crown jewel of mortalkind, hence its name. Encased in a crystalline, near-spherical bubble sprawling just shy of 5000 km2, and another 30 km up into the sky, it is an impenetrable fortress. Its borders are lined with stone walls older than the calendar, the parabola of its artificial sky stands upon a massive pillar system that by themselves serve as nexii for life and civilization. Each of the four pillars bear a name, and dictate a cardinal direction: Ferris being north, Halcyon marking south, Medea west, and Sindi east.

The only connection the city has with the outside world is the Kirkolisch river: an ice cold, clear water stream that enters under the walls from the east, and pools in the middle of the city in the Crystal Eye, Lake Augusta. Kirkolisch's dam is a common place for the curious few's attempted escapes, and while rumors exist of successes, none have ever returned, or even provided a sign of life. Magic or not, it is deceptively simple to drown in a stream of that force.

The repellent field, or as it is colloquially known, the "bubble", or "dome", has stood since time immemorial. Following the outbreak of The Cataclysm, it was erected by the prayers of the spiteful. Often it is said, it does not exist to keep the outside world outside; rather, it is meant to keep whatever is in the city, inside the city. Or, so goes the mythos of it. Craiul exists shunned by the outside world, and it's what protects its sovereignty as well. The dome's painfully cold walls, its crystalline structure, its mere robustness, has stood the test of time. Although the government has personnel dedicated to its maintenance, hardly as much as an emergency has reared its head since the bubble's existence.
