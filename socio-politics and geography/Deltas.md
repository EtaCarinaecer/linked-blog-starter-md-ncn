Craiul, for both economic, social, historical, and navigational purposes, is separated into Deltas, or sectors. These were once smaller regions and villages that sprawled into the main city upon their isolation from the outside world, but wished to maintain some form of autonomy.

Although they each have their own unique names, usually only people local to that particular region call it that, whilst everyone else simply refers to it by number.
